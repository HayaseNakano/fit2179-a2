This is not just an income gap — it is a geographic one.

All states have seen income growth since 1970, but KL's median income grew ~40× while Kelantan's grew only ~18×. Gini coefficients remain stubbornly high in Sabah and Kelantan, indicating persistent internal inequality even within poorer states.
